Monolake

2015-11-04 · Amazing Places

We saw a very large inviting looking lake from a restaurant close by. When we drove down to the lake, we saw a lot of black spots everywhere on the lake, can you guess what it is? We were very surprised to find out that there were millions of flys. I think that was the reason why we did not stay there very long. It was a lot of buzzing around you. A very interesting experience.
