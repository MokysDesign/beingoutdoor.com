Bodie - Ghost Town

2015-11-04 · Amazing Places

I always wanted to go to see a ghost town, walking around, taking pictures and wondering what the story of the people who lived there was. But it came different then expected. There is a 3mi unpaved dirt road which brings you to Bodie, for some reason it took us a very long time to get there. Finally arrived we learned that we only have 15min left until they close. I took my camera and only make it to the first few houses. I hope I will go back on a not so hot day with more time.
