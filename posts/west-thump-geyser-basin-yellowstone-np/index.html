West Thump Geyser Basin: Yellowstone NP

2016-01-06 · Yellowstone NP

This was a very easy and short hike to see a variety of geothermal basins. The walk is on wooden walkways and you get very close to the basins. You even see hot water bubbling in the yellowstone lake.
