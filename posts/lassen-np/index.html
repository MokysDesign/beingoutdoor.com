Lassen NP

2015-10-01 · Lassen Volcanic NP, National Parks

Lassen is a Volcanic National Park, with lots of volcanoes, meadows, forests and mountain lakes. You are able to see a lot of the park just by driving through and doing short hikes. There is also a Lake with Kayak rental close by.
