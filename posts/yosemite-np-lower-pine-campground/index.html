Yosemite NP: Lower Pine Campground

2015-09-15 · Campgrounds

Yosemite Valley Campgrounds book out really quick. The lower pine campground is large with little to no privacy. I would try to get a site more in the back of the campground, otherwise everyone who comes in and out, passes your tent. You have a lot of RVs with generators. If you like easy camping, with restaurants and stores close by the campgrounds are ok. Yosemite valley is always crowded. - Would I go there again? No, I rather would go to camp 4, a little farther west, but since it is a walk in campground, there are no RV's.
