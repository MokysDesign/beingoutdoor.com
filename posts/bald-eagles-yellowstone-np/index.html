Bald Eagles - Yellowstone NP

2016-02-09 · Yellowstone NP

I never saw a bald Eagle before and was hoping that we would see at least one. It was an amazing and exciting moment, seeing these birds. We stopped at this meadow, because there were lots of people, waiting at dusk for the wolves to come out. I was so glad we talked to them! This was our view. I did not expect to see a bald eagle sitting in a meadow. I learned on this trip, the more you talk to other people, the more you see. We met one family sitting and waiting with binoculars, we ask them what they were waiting for, and they let us see through their binoculars. It was focused on a peregrine falcon nest with chicks!
