Mount Tamalpais SP: Campground Steep Ravine

2015-10-12 · Campgrounds, Mount Tamalpais SP, State Park

If you like to stay on this campground, you need to book early. There are only 7 Campsite, every site has ocean view. Some sites have shade, some are in full sun. You can also rent a cabin, which is very close to the private beach. The campground is away from the street, and you can only drive down the road if you have a reservation and know the lock code at the gate.
