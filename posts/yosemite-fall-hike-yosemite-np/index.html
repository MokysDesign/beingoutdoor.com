Yosemite Fall Hike: Yosemite NP

2015-09-25 · Hikes

This is another hike with a lot of switch backs. You get nice views of the valley and sometimes of black bears (we saw one). The last part of the trail is exposed to the sun. There are a lot of little ponds up there to dip your feed in (far away from the edge). At least when I was there in fall with not so much water....
