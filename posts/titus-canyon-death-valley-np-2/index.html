Titus Canyon: Death Valley NP

2015-09-18 · DeathValley NP

You can drive through Titus Canyon in Death Valley, it is a 27 mi long dirt road. It takes a couple of hours, since it is beautiful and you can not drive very fast, you want to do a lot of stops and enjoy the scenery. You drive along a ghost town, narrow canyons and you'll see petroglyphs.
