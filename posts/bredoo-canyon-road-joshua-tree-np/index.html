Bredoo Canyon Road: Joshua Tree NP

2015-09-22 · Joshua Tree NP

We never tried a dirt road before, and were so exited to made it over the sand road to the canyon entrance. When we looked down to see how the road looked like, we decided not to go any further. I would have loved to see it, but we were not prepared enough for that. It was still a great drive through the park.
