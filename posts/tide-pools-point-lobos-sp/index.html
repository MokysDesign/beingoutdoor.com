Tide Pools: Point Lobos SP

2015-09-30 · Point Lobos SP

My kids love walking around tide pools and looking for animals. Point Lobos is a great place for that. You don't need to hike to get there, the parking lot is very close by. We also saw a lot of Whales passing by.
