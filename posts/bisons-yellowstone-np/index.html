Bisons: Yellowstone NP

2016-01-06 · Yellowstone NP

You get ready to go to Yellowstone and people tell you, there are so many Bisons..... We entered the park, and saw none! After two days my son pointed out of the window: "Mom is that a Bison? Is it real?" And it was.
This was the first we saw...
...then we were happy to see one on a meadow....
... on the street in front of us...
...a herd with a baby...
... then we realized they are everywhere. Each dark spot is a Bison.
