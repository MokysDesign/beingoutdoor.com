Great Fountain Geyser: Yellowstone NP

2016-01-20 · Yellowstone NP

The Great Fountain Geyser, is off the main road, and not so crowded as a lot of other places in Yellowstone. I don't know how busy it can be when it goes off...... It was beautiful to stay there and watch the moving clouds mirroring in the water.
