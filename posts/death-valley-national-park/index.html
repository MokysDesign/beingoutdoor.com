Death Valley NP

2015-09-17 · DeathValley NP, National Parks

Death Valley National Park is worth to go there. There is so much to explore and see. I never thought that a dessert can be so colorful. You can go hiking in a lot of different canyons. Drive through a canyon or to the sailing rocks. Run over sand dunes. The best time to go is spring and fall. There will be some more blog entries about specific thinks I did there.
