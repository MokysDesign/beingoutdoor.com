Joshua Tree NP: Campground Indian Cove

2015-09-22 · Campgrounds, Joshua Tree NP

This is a large Campground, on the north side of Joshua Tree Park. You can make reservations in advance. The sites are very open and you don't have a lot of privacy. It is nice to have stores close by, but it is is a 40 min drive into the park. Would I go again: yes, especially when I think it could be hard to get a spot inside the park.
