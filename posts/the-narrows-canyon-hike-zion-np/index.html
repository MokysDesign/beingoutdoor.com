The Narrows Canyon hike: Zion NP

2015-09-21 · Hikes, Zion NP

We went to Zion NP on our way to the Grand Canyon, we planned on being one day in the park. Basically we drove through with a lot of stops, and did one hike. Be prepared for this hike, you are going to walk in the water a lot, one area was about 1m deep. I was happy to wear shoes which covered my toes. I also used hiking poles, they saved me a couple times from getting out of balance and falling in the water completely. I carried one of my boys on my back, he was able to stay dry all the time. This is a park where I want to go back to explore more.
