Mesquite spring campground: Death Valley NP

2015-09-21 · Campgrounds, DeathValley NP

This campground is at the north part of Death Valley, very close to zu Uhebe Crater. The sites are very open, you don't have privacy, and sun the whole day. There is a river bed next to the campground, it was dry when we were there in april.
