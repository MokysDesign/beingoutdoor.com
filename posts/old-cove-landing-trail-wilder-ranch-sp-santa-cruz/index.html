Wilder Ranch SP: Old Cove Landing Trail

2015-09-30 · Hikes, State Park, Wilder Ranch SP

This 2.5 mi hike shows you similar views like you get when you drive down coast highway 1, but the trail is away from the street. There are great spots for a picknick and Whale watching.
