Sailing Rocks and Tea Cattle Junction in Death Valley NP

2015-09-17 · DeathValley NP

It took us 2h, to drive down the off road to the sailing rocks. It was amazing to see these large rocks with their scratch marks in the dried out lake. Even during Spring, it was very hot. Just be aware that there are rattlesnakes, we heard one closer to the mountains.
