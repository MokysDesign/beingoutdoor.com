Tenaya Lake: Yosemite NP

2015-09-25

You can not drive up Tioga pass without a stop at Tenaya Lake. There are beautiful beaches, and the water is always refreshing. It is a great place to make a stop for a picknick or just for relax and enjoy the surroundings. Kayaking on the lake is also a lot of fun.
