Black Sand Basin - Yellowstone NP

2016-01-14 · Yellowstone NP

Black Sand Basin is a great Area to see geothermal ponds in different colors, small geysers and hot water floating into the river, without having too many people around you.
the wooden walk ways bring you from one nice area to the next
hot water flows into the river
