Old Faithful - Yellowstone NP

2016-01-13 · Yellowstone NP

Went to  Yellowstone in June, because I read that July and August will be very busy. Well it was already very busy in June, and we did not have fun finding parking at Old Faithful. However, it is a very beautiful area to do small or longer hikes. We saw Old Faithful going off 3 Times.
