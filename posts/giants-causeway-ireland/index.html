Giant's Causeway: Ireland

2015-10-13 · Amazing Places

If you visit Ireland, you definitely want to go to the giants causeway. It is about a 15min walk from the parking lot to the ocean, but they also have busses who can bring you down there. It is very impressive to see these large basalt columns going into the ocean, building little hills to climb on and also finding them in the surrounding Cliffs. Regardless of the weather, it is worth a trip.
