Grand Canyon NP

2015-10-02 · Grand Canyon NP, National Parks

Grand Canyon National Park was our second Road Trip with both kids. For me a Road trip is a tour, longer then 3 nights and more then 1000mi. We only had a couple of days, but I think the pictures say enough. We hiked down the south kaibab trail down until Skeleton Point (without kids). You get great views inside the Canyon. We enjoyed the sunset at Yaki point.
