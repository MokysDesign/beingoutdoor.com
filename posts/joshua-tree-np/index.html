Joshua Tree NP

2015-09-22 · Joshua Tree NP, National Parks

Joshua Tree National Park was the second National Park I went to after starting our camping trips. The campgrounds inside the Park are "first come first serve". We were lucky, we went over Memorial Day weekend, arrived at about 12pm, and got one of the last spots. You can make a reservation at  Indian Cove campground on the north side of the park, if you like to know wether you have a spot or not.
