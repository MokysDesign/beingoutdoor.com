Hetch Hetchy: Yosemite NP

2015-09-29 · Hikes

It is hard to believe that Hetch Hetchy was once a Valley like Yosemity Valley. There is one day Trail along the lake, we hiked until we reached the waterfall. We started on a day with clouds, when we reached the waterfall we had rain and hail. This is a very easy hike.
