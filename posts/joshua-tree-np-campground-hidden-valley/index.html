Joshua Tree NP: Campground Hidden Valley

2015-09-22 · Campgrounds, Joshua Tree NP

This is a first come first serve campground, with very private sites. You have Rocks and plants around the campsites, with small paths to your neighbors. You can go and start climbing and walking around the huge rocks right from your campsite. Be aware that there is no water on this campground. Would I go again? Yes, the night sky was amazing and you are in the middle of the park.
