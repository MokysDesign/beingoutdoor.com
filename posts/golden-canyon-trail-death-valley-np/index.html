Golden Canyon Trail: Death Valley NP

2015-09-18 · DeathValley NP, Hikes

This is an easy hike, since you are in the dessert, start early and bring plenty of water. There is not much shade on this hiking. It is an easy hike, mostly flat, with a lot of different shaped canyons around you. We saw one roadrunner. You can also drive to Zabriskie Point to see the Canyon from the top.
