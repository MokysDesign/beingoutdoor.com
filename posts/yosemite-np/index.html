Yosemite NP

2015-09-23 · National Parks

Yosemite National Park is my favorite National Park. The views even just from driving through the park are amazing. Most of the times I visited I was in the Valley, now I try to go to a different area and do different hikes. My most success in Yosemite was, being on top of Half Dome.
