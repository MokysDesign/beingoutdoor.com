Yosemite NP in white

2015-09-29 · National Parks

Even in Winter, Yosemite is a great place to go. It is much more quiet and not as busy as in the summer month. The kids loved it to get pulled on the sled and you should bring spikes for your shoes. They also sell them in the stores in the Valley.
