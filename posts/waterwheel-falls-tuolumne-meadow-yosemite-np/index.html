Waterwheel Falls: Yosemite NP

2015-09-29 · Hikes

This hike shows you how diverse Yosemite can be. You hike over meadows, through forests, along the Tuolumne Rive, over large flat rock areas and down waterfalls. You can go swimming in the river, the only part I did not like is, that you hike downhill first, and the way back is uphill.
