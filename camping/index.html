Camping Set-Ups

The vehicles and the kit — short version: each set-up tells you which trips on this site it's good for.

Short version: Valley of Fire works in any stock SUV or truck. Calico wants a stock high-clearance 4x4 (a lifted rig with low range only if you're doing the rock crawls). White Rim requires true high-clearance 4WD with low range — park rules, no AWD crossovers. Lost Coast needs high clearance, with 4x4 strongly recommended for Usal Road in the dry season.

The set-ups

Truck Pickup with a rooftop tent or bed camper — hauls all the gear and handles every trip on this site. Jeep Rubicon · Lifted Low range, lockers and clearance — the set-up for White Rim and the rock-crawl loops. SUV A stock high-clearance SUV covers Valley of Fire and the main trails — tent camping from the trunk. Camper Van Comfort at basecamp — great for the developed campgrounds; leave the rough trails to a 4x4.
