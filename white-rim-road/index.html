TRIP 03 - CANYONLANDS, UTAH - ~850 MILES - 4-5 DAYS - HIGH-CLEARANCE 4WD

White Rim Road - Canyonlands

A 100-mile loop around the Island in the Sky mesa in Canyonlands National Park - one of the most spectacular multi-day 4x4 routes in the American West.

DISTANCE

~850 mi

DAYS

4-5 days

VEHICLE NEEDED

HC 4WD + low range

BEST SEASON

DOGS

Not Allowed

Camping & Permits

Camping on the White Rim is permit-only backcountry - your overnight permit IS your campsite reservation, so this is the first thing to book.

Overnight PermitBook on recreation.gov up to 4 months ahead — spring and fall weekends go instantly. Book this FIRST. Designated Sites Only10 camping areas / 20 sites along the route — no dispersed camping, no water, pack-out toilet system required. White CrackBest halfway camp on a 2-day run — sweeping views toward the Needles district. Murphy Hogback & GooseberryAlternative 2-day camps — Murphy sits on the rim after a steep climb, 360° mesa views. Labyrinth CampsAdd these Green River overlook sites for a 3-day itinerary. Before & AfterMoab hotels, or Horsethief Campground on the mesa top near the Mineral Bottom exit.

Activities

Shafer Trail SwitchbacksThe dramatic 1,400 ft descent that starts the loop. Musselman ArchWalk across the roadside arch — sheer drops on both sides. Lathrop CanyonSide road down to the Colorado River — great lunch spot. StargazingCanyonlands is a certified International Dark Sky Park. Mesa-Top SunriseGreen River Overlook or Mesa Arch — before or after the loop. Monument BasinPhotography stop — towering spires straight out of a western.

Hikes & Viewpoints Along the Route

Hiking trail links via AllTrails. Full White Rim road guide on Trails Offroad.

White Rim Overlook Trail1.8 mi RT · easy — flat trail to a mesa-edge overlook with Colorado River views; best late afternoon. Fort Bottom Ruin Trail3.4 mi RT · moderate · 511 ft gain — ancient ruin and Green River viewpoint, from Fort Bottom campsite. Monument Basin ViewpointRoadside — towering sandstone spires rising from the canyon floor. Musselman ArchRoadside — natural sandstone arch you can walk across, sheer-drop canyon views. White CrackRoadside campsite — the best halfway stop, views toward the Needles. Murphy HogbackShort steep climb — rim campsites with 360° mesa views.

Tips & Logistics

Base in MoabGreat restaurants, gear shops and hotels before and after the loop. Vehicle RequirementsHigh-clearance 4WD with low range mandatory — Wrangler/4Runner-class minimum; no AWD crossovers, no ATVs/UTVs. All-terrain tires highly recommended. Height limit 10 ft. No Water on RouteBring everything you need for the full loop. No Cell ServiceDownload offline maps before dropping in. Travel in GroupsA tow off the White Rim can cost $2,000+. Shafer Canyon EntrySteep switchbacks; impassable when wet — check conditions with the park first. Avoid SummerCanyon temps exceed 110°F — go Mar–May or Sep–Nov.
