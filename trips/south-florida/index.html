South Florida

Miami, Key West, the Everglades, and a rocket launch at Kennedy Space Center — one trip, four stops.

South Florida in one trip is a different shape than a road trip or a city break — the distances are short, the stops are wildly different from each other, and the through-line is the flight down and a few days of variety. This is the trip page for that window: Miami as a base, day trips south to Key West and west into the Everglades, and a drive up the coast to Kennedy Space Center to see a rocket launch.

Each stop below links back to its place on the homepage, where the longer write-up will live. For now this page is the index for the trip.
