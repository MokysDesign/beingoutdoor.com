City

City hubs still to visit or come back to.

Hub Boston Boston Walkable history — the Freedom Trail, harbor islands, and the start of a New England road trip north. Hub Las Vegas Las Vegas Basecamp city — drive in, sleep there, drive out to Valley of Fire, Death Valley, Grand Canyon, Zion. Hub Los Angeles Los Angeles Neighborhoods, beaches, and the drives between them — LA rewards a car and a few extra days. Hub Munich Munich Done — beer halls, the Englischer Garten, and the Alps an hour away. Hub Oslo Oslo A city that opens straight into fjord and forest — the launchpad for a Norway trip. Hub Paris Paris Done — neighborhoods over landmarks, day trips to Versailles and Giverny. Hub Salzburg Salzburg Done — old town, the river, and the surrounding lakes and mountains. Hub San Diego San Diego Best weather in the country — Balboa Park, the coast, and easy access to the desert in under two hours. Hub San Francisco San Francisco Done — neighborhoods, day trips down the coast, and the drives that make it worth more than a weekend. Hub Seattle Seattle Pike Place, ferries, and the Olympics on the doorstep — a basecamp for rainforest and mountain day trips. Hub Tokyo Tokyo Neighborhoods over landmarks — Shibuya, Shimokitazawa, day trips to Kamakura and Hakone. Hub Vienna Vienna Done — cafés, music halls, and the Wienerwald on the edge of town. Hub Zurich Zurich Done — old town, lake, and the Alps an hour away.
