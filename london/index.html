Day 1 · Arrival & Thames Walk

A packed first day on foot, in the order we actually walked it: a morning coffee, then the covered markets - Old Spitalfields, Leadenhall, and Borough - lunch at the Art Nouveau Blackfriar, Lower Marsh market, the Leake Street graffiti tunnel, and the London Eye, ending along the South Bank at the Oxo Tower and dinner at The Alchemist Bevis Marks.
Walked: 8.8 miles.

Spitalfields MarketOld Spitalfields - a covered market of food stalls, vintage, and crafts. Leadenhall MarketThe ornate Victorian arcade - a Diagon Alley filming spot. Borough MarketLunch stop - London's best-loved food market. The BlackfriarA jewel-box Art Nouveau pub by Blackfriars - marble, mosaics, and lunch on the terrace. Lower MarshWaterloo’s characterful street market - indie food stalls and vintage. The Graffiti TunnelLeake Street Arches - London's legal street-art tunnel under Waterloo. London EyeThe riverside big wheel - panoramic views over the Thames. The Alchemist Bevis MarksA theatrical cocktail bar to end the day, back in the City.

Day 2 · Bletchley Park

The codebreaker day - ~40 minutes by train from Euston to Bletchley Park (Hut 8, The Mansion, and The National Museum of Computing) - then back to London for Camden Lock Market and an evening in the pubs.
Walked: 6.4 miles.

Bletchley ParkThe WWII codebreaking estate: Hut 8, the Mansion, and more, ~40 min by train from Euston. The National Museum of ComputingThe working Bombe and Colossus rebuilds, same campus. Camden Lock MarketBack in London - canal-side stalls and street food.
↑ Back to Day by Day

Day 3 · Dover

Train from St Pancras to Dover (~1 hr): the White Cliffs and Dover Castle with its Secret Wartime Tunnels, then back to London for dinner at Dishoom Shoreditch and a pub.
Walked: 11.5 miles.

White Cliffs of DoverThe clifftop walk past Langdon Beach - France on the horizon. Dover CastleThe great keep and the Secret Wartime Tunnels (Operation Dynamo) - book online for 10% off. Dishoom ShoreditchBombay-style dinner back in London - bacon naan rolls and black daal. The MinoriesA traditional pub by Tower Hill to round off the day.
↑ Back to Day by Day

Day 4 · Westminster & the West End

Westminster landmarks into the West End: Big Ben and Westminster Abbey, Trafalgar and Leicester Squares, shopping on Carnaby and Oxford Street (with the giant LEGO store), The Clink, and drinks with a view at Sky Garden.
Walked: 7.8 miles.

Big BenThe clock tower and Houses of Parliament, best from the riverside. Westminster AbbeyThe coronation church, almost 1,000 years of royal history. Trafalgar SquareNelson's Column and the lions, with the National Gallery next door. LEGO Store Leicester SquareThe world’s biggest LEGO store, with M&M’s World next door. Carnaby StreetIndie fashion and streetwear in Soho's most colorful lane. SohoWandering the West End’s liveliest quarter - bars, cafés, and neon. Oxford & Regent StreetThe flagship shopping mile - Selfridges, Nike Town and every brand you can name. The Clink Prison MuseumOn the site of one of England’s oldest prisons, on Bankside (ticketed). Sky GardenRooftop garden 155 m up - free entry (book ahead), bars and restaurants at the top.
↑ Back to Day by Day

Day 5 · HMS Belfast & the Dungeon

A riverside day by the Thames: the London Dungeon and HMS Belfast, Tower Bridge, and a stretch of the Roman London Wall - finishing with an evening walk around the Tower of London and Tower Bridge lit up.
Walked: 7.1 miles.

The London DungeonTheatrical, spooky-fun ride through London’s dark history. HMS BelfastWWII cruiser moored by Tower Bridge, nine decks to explore. Tower BridgeThe Victorian bascule bridge - up close on the evening riverside loop. London WallSurviving stretches of the Roman city wall near Tower Hill.
↑ Back to Day by Day

Day 6 · Palace, War Rooms & Museum

A royal-and-history day: Buckingham Palace and the Changing of the Guard, a wander through St James’s Park with a café stop, the Churchill War Rooms, and the British Museum - ending with a night walk along the Thames.
Walked: 7.4 miles.

Buckingham PalaceThe exterior and the free Changing of the Guard (most days ~11:00). St James’s ParkThe royal park’s lake and pelicans, with a lakeside café stop - the classic view back to the palace. Churchill War RoomsThe untouched WWII bunker under Westminster. The British MuseumRosetta Stone, mummies, the Parthenon sculptures. Free. Thames Night WalkAn evening stroll along the river - the city lit up on the water.
↑ Back to Day by Day

Day 7 · The Tower & the West End

The last full day: Covent Garden, the Tower of London, dinner at SUSHISAMBA, and Les Misérables in the West End to finish - with Piccadilly Circus lit up along the way.
Walked: 6.5 miles.

Covent GardenThe piazza and covered market - street performers and the Apple Market. Tower of LondonCrown Jewels, Beefeaters, and ravens (best early afternoon). SUSHISAMBA LondonDinner out - Japanese-Brazilian-Peruvian sharing plates. Les MisérablesThe world’s longest-running musical at the Sondheim Theatre (Delfont Mackintosh). Our evening finale. Piccadilly CircusThe bright lights - the West End’s neon crossroads.
↑ Back to Day by Day

Day 8 · St Paul’s Cathedral

A final morning before heading home: St Paul’s Cathedral and lunch at Gordon Ramsay’s Bread Street Kitchen & Bar.
Walked: 5.3 miles.

St. Paul’s CathedralWren’s dome - the climb to the Whispering Gallery and city views. Bread Street Kitchen & BarGordon Ramsay’s all-day restaurant at One New Change, right by St Paul’s.
