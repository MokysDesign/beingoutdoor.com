Day 1 · Mon Jul 26 · KEF → Reykjavík

🚗 Total driving: ~48 mi · ~1.1 h

Arrival day — grab the rental at KEF, cross the Reykjanes rift into Reykjavík, then hit the city's best bits and finish with an evening soak at Sky Lagoon.

Bridge Between Continents⏱ 20 min · Walk the rift between the two tectonic plates.Sky Lagoon⏱ 2 h · Geothermal spa ritual — booking.Hallgrímskirkja⏱ 45 min · Iconic church; tower for the city view.Rainbow Street + Laugavegur⏱ 45 min · Rainbow-painted street rising to the church, plus the main shopping street.Harpa + Old Harbour⏱ 45 min · Glass concert hall and the harbour.🏛️Settlement Exhibition⏱ 1 h · Underground Viking longhouse museum.

Day 2 · Tue Jul 27 · Golden Circle → Hella

🚗 Total driving: ~162 mi · ~4.3 h

The classic Golden Circle loop, then south toward Hella for the night.

Þingvellir⏱ 1 h · Rift valley & oldest parliament.Kerið Crater⏱ 45 min · Volcanic crater lake.Strokkur / Geysir⏱ 30 min · Erupts every ~8 minutes.Gullfoss⏱ 30 min · The two-tier 'Golden Falls'.Friðheimar⏱ 1 h · Greenhouse tomato-farm lunch — book.Secret Lagoon⏱ 1.5 h · Oldest natural pool — optional.

Day 3 · Wed Jul 28 · Landmannalaugar

🚗 Total driving: ~8 mi · ~15 min (own) — tour driving is the guide's

Highlands day by super-jeep from Hvolsvöllur — rhyolite mountains, a hike, and a soak in the natural hot river.

Super-jeep tour (Midgard)⏱ 8–9 h · Guided F-road trip — book.Highland hike⏱ 2 h · Brennisteinsalda loop.Hot river soak⏱ 1.5 h · Bathe in the natural geothermal stream.

Day 4 · Thu Jul 29 · South Coast → Vík

🚗 Total driving: ~71 mi · ~2.0 h

Waterfall crawl along the south coast into Vík — save the ice cave for tomorrow morning.

Seljalandsfoss⏱ 45 min · Walk BEHIND the falls.Gljúfrabúi⏱ 20 min · Hidden falls in a slot canyon.Skógafoss⏱ 45 min · Big 60 m curtain.🏛️Skógar Museum⏱ 1–1.5 h · Folk & transport museum — optional.Sólheimajökull⏱ 30–45 min · Glacier tongue — optional.Dyrhólaey⏱ 30 min · Arch headland; puffins.Reynisfjara⏱ 45 min · Black sand + basalt columns.

Day 5 · Fri Jul 30 · Vík → Jökulsárlón

🚗 Total driving: ~109 mi · ~2.6 h

Ice cave by super-jeep in the morning, then east past the canyon to Skaftafell — overnight at the boat-tour area (Fosshotel).

Katla Ice Cave⏱ 3 h · Morning ice-cave tour from Vík — book.Fjaðrárgljúfur⏱ 45 min · 100 m-deep canyon rim walk.Svartifoss / Skaftafell⏱ 1.5–2 h · Basalt-column 'Black Falls' hike.

Day 6 · Sat Jul 31 · Jökulsárlón → Djúpivogur

🚗 Total driving: ~135 mi · ~5.0 h

Speedboat among the icebergs mid-morning, then Diamond Beach and the Vestrahorn dunes on the way east — overnight in Djúpivogur.

Fjallsárlón⏱ 30 min · Quieter glacier lagoon.Jökulsárlón zodiac⏱ 2 h · Mid-morning zodiac among icebergs — book.Diamond Beach⏱ 30 min · Icebergs on black sand.Vestrahorn / Stokksnes⏱ 1–1.5 h · The classic mountain-and-dunes shot.

Day 7 · Sun Aug 1 · Djúpivogur → Seyðisfjörður

🚗 Total driving: ~110 mi · ~2.6 h

East Fjords day — the Djúpivogur eggs, quiet fishing villages, and the artsy town of Seyðisfjörður.

Djúpivogur⏱ 30 min · Quiet fjord village.Eggin í Gleðivík⏱ 15 min · The 34 egg sculptures on the harbour.🏛️Petra's Stone Collection⏱ 1–1.5 h · Private mineral museum.The Blue Church⏱ 30 min · Seyðisfjörður's blue church.

Day 8 · Mon Aug 2 · Dettifoss → Mývatn

🚗 Total driving: ~186 mi · ~4.4 h

Over the highlands to the north — Europe's most powerful waterfall, a horseshoe canyon, and a geothermal soak.

Dettifoss⏱ 45 min · Europe's most powerful waterfall.Ásbyrgi canyon⏱ 1 h · Horseshoe canyon.Mývatn Nature Baths⏱ 1.5–2 h · Geothermal soak — book.

Day 9 · Tue Aug 3 · Mývatn + Húsavík

🚗 Total driving: ~110 mi · ~3.4 h

A geothermal loop around Lake Mývatn, then whale watching out of Húsavík.

Hverir⏱ 30 min · Boiling mud pots & fumaroles.Krafla⏱ 30 min · Víti crater + power station.Grjótagjá⏱ 15 min · Lava cave with a geothermal pool.Dimmuborgir⏱ 45 min · 'Dark castles' lava formations.Skútustadagirg⏱ 20 min · Lakeside pseudo-craters.Húsavík whale watching⏱ 3 h · Oak-boat tour — book.

Day 10 · Wed Aug 4 · Mývatn → Snæfellsnes

🚗 Total driving: ~295 mi · ~7 h

The long transfer west — Goðafoss, a stop in Akureyri, then across to Snæfellsnes.

Goðafoss⏱ 30–45 min · 'Waterfall of the Gods'.Akureyri⏱ 1 h · Lunch + Brynja ice cream.

Day 11 · Thu Aug 5 · Snæfellsnes → Reykjavík

🚗 Total driving: ~169 mi · ~4.2 h

Snæfellsnes loop back to the capital — cliff walks, a black-pebble beach, and Kirkjufell.

Arnarstapi cliff walk⏱ 1 h · Cliffs + the Gatklettur arch.Djúpalónssandur⏱ 30–45 min · Black-pebble beach + lifting stones.Kirkjufell⏱ 30–45 min · The arrow mountain + falls.

Hikes — Robin’s shortlist

Four trail ideas for Robin, with a quick read on whether our rental car can reach each trailhead. (Same access notes are on the map above — click a 🥾 pin.)

Þakgil — Austurafréttur loop
Near Vík · Road 214 (Kerlingardalsvegur), ~30 min
Short marked red/yellow loop, ~2–4 km — good evening/morning leg-stretch
🚗 Gravel road 214 (~15 km) — careful 2WD in dry weather; check rental policy
Fremri-Fellsfoss
Above Skógafoss · Fimmvörðuháls trail
~5.7 mi / ~3–4 h round trip — a string of waterfalls up the Skógá gorge
🚗 Easy — Skógafoss car park, right on the Ring Road (paved)
Stórurð
East Fjords · Road 94 (Vatnsskarð / Borgarfjörður Eystri)
~13 km / ~4–5 h round trip — giant boulders + turquoise pools (best mid-Jul on)
🚗 Road 94, 2WD fine — confirm live summer status
Laugavegur Trail
Landmannalaugar → Þórsmörk · south highlands
~54 km, 4-day hut trek (or a taster leg from Landmannalaugar)
🚗 F-road (F208/F225), 4×4 only — jeep access, not our rental

Note: Icelandic rentals usually exclude F-road — and often gravel-road — damage on 2WD vehicles. Verify the contract before any unmade road.
