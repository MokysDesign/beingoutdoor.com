Attractions

Brandenburg GateBerlin's most famous landmark - the neoclassical gate at Pariser Platz. Reichstag BuildingThe German parliament with the Norman Foster glass dome - free, but book ahead. Berlin CathedralThe neo-Renaissance cathedral on Museum Island - climb the dome for river views. Charlottenburg PalaceBerlin's largest palace - baroque rooms, gardens, and the Neue Pavilion (placeholder photo). Potsdamer PlatzThe redeveloped square that was no-man's-land during the Cold War - Sony Center's tensile roof.
