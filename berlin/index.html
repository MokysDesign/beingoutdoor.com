Attractions

Brandenburg GateBerlin's most famous landmark - the neoclassical gate at Pariser Platz. Reichstag BuildingThe German parliament with the Norman Foster glass dome - free, but book ahead. Berlin CathedralThe neo-Renaissance cathedral on Museum Island - climb the dome for river views. Charlottenburg PalaceBerlin's largest palace - baroque rooms, gardens, and the Neue Pavilion. Potsdamer PlatzThe redeveloped square that was no-man's-land during the Cold War - Sony Center's tensile roof.

East German icons like the Trabant are on display. Jewish Museum BerlinDaniel Libeskind's zinc-clad building and the axes of German-Jewish history. Die Mauer PanoramaA 60-m painted panorama showing the Wall's construction, near Checkpoint Charlie.

Architecture

Berlin is an outdoor museum of 20th-century architecture - Bauhaus, postwar reconstruction, Cold War icons, and the new Potsdamer Platz quarter that rebuilt on no-man's-land. Here are the buildings worth pointing your camera at.

Bauhaus DessauThe Gropius-designed school building - 1.5 hr by train, day-trip from Berlin. ReichstagNorman Foster's glass-and-mirror dome capping the 1894 parliament - free, book ahead. Jewish Museum BerlinDaniel Libeskind's deconstructivist zinc-clad zigzag - the icon of post-Wall Berlin architecture. Sony CenterHelmut Jahn's tent-roof forum at Potsdamer Platz - tensile canopy and mountain-like peaks. Berlin Cathedral1905 neo-Renaissance cathedral with a 75 m copper dome - the largest Protestant church in Germany. FernsehturmThe 368 m TV tower at Alexanderplatz - GDR-built, still Germany's tallest structure. Charlottenburg Palace1699 baroque royal palace, the largest in the city - rooms and gardens open to the public. Haus der Kulturen der Welt1956 former congress hall by Hugh Stubbins, the Cold-War gift from the US - now an arts venue. Neues MuseumDavid Chipperfield's restoration weaving the new into the war-damaged original - Nefertiti lives here. Konzerthaus BerlinSchinkel's 1821 Schauspielhaus rebuilt by Stüler after WWII - the neoclassical home of the Konzerthausorchester.

Food & Drink

Markthalle NeunCross-cultural market hall in Kreuzberg - currywurst, cheese, Thursday Street Food Thursday. Mustafa's Gemüse KebapFamous late-night veg-loaded döner on Mehringdamm - expect a queue. DönerA Berlin invention - every neighborhood has its spot; look for Imbiss with a vertical spit. Schnitzel & FriesWiener or Jägerschnitzel with fries - a German/Austrian classic, served everywhere. Spaghetti Ice CreamPress vanilla ice cream through a spätzle press, top with strawberry sauce and white chocolate 'parmesan'. EiskaffeeIced coffee with a scoop of vanilla ice and whipped cream - a classic Berlin café order.

History & Cold War

Checkpoint CharlieThe famous Cold War crossing point on Friedrichstraße, now a museum (placeholder photo). Berlin Wall MemorialPreserved Wall and watchtower along Bernauer Straße with an open-air exhibit. East Side GalleryLongest preserved stretch of the Wall, painted with murals - including 'My God, Help Me to Survive'. Memorial to the Murdered JewsEisenman's 2,711 concrete stelae near Brandenburg Gate, plus the underground 'Place of Information'. Topography of TerrorFree outdoor and indoor exhibit on the former Gestapo site, on Niederkirchner Straße. German ChancelleryThe federal government's seat - modest looking from outside, vast courtyard inside.
