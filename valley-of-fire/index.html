Valley of Fire State Park

Nevada's oldest state park - blazing red Aztec sandstone formations, ancient petroglyphs, and alien landscapes just an hour from Las Vegas.

Camping Options

Two campgrounds inside the park, both surrounded by red rock - or fall back to Las Vegas for a no-camp version.

Atlatl Rock CampgroundIn-park with hookups — ~44 sites at the base of the petroglyph rock; power & water, showers, dump station. Book on Reserve Nevada — winter/spring weekends fill fast. Arch Rock CampgroundIn-park primitive — smaller and quieter, sites tucked into red sandstone coves. No hookups — the more scenic tent choice. Las Vegas BaseHotel fallback 1 hr from the park — good for shoulder-season heat or a shower night.

Activities

Scenic DriveNV-169 Scenic Byway and Mouse's Tank Road. Petroglyphs at Atlatl RockAncient Native American rock art up a staircase viewing platform. Wildlife SpottingBighorn sheep, roadrunners, kit foxes. Sunrise & Sunset PhotographyThe best light in the park — golden hour on red rock. Lake Mead Day TripNational Recreation Area, 30 min east.

Hikes

Five Canyon Overlook1 mi · easy · ~45 min — sandy trail to the Fire Canyon overlook, best in late-afternoon light. Rainbow Vista Trail1 mi · easy · ~25 min — colorful sandstone bowl with 360° canyon views. Open year-round. Petroglyph Canyon / Mouse's Tank0.7 mi · easy · ~30–45 min — narrow red canyon packed with petroglyphs. Silica Dome0.9 mi · easy · ~22 min — light scramble to the white dome, views over Fire Canyon and Lake Mead. Fire Wave & Pink Canyon2 mi · easy · ~50 min — iconic swirling slickrock plus pink slot canyon. Best at sunrise. Closed Jun–Oct. White Domes Trail1.1 mi · moderate · ~30–45 min — loop through a slot canyon and 1966 movie-set ruins. Hike clockwise. Closed mid-May–Sep.

Tips & Logistics

Entry Fee$15/vehicle for non-NV residents. Start EarlyOn the trail by 7am in shoulder seasons. WaterBring 3L+ per person per hike. Offline MapDownload the NV State Parks offline map before arriving. Fuel UpGas up before entering — no services inside the park. Avoid SummerJune–August regularly hits 110°F+ — plan Oct–Apr.
