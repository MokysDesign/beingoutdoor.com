Hi

I love traveling and exploring the world. You always find me behind my camera. If there is snow I probably will be on the mountain. If there is time I build out our camping setups.

This site is where I keep track of it all.

Travel diary

Every trip, with what the drive was like, where we stayed, and what I would do again. Some entries are detailed (London, eight days). Some are quick (one-night campgrounds). When I am planning a new trip I write the itinerary here first so I do not lose it.

Camping setups

I build my own: rooftop tents, drawer systems, the kind of stuff that lives in the truck. Posts about those are coming soon.

Photography
