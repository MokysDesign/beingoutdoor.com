About this blog....

Being outdoor and going camping is one of the most important things I like to do. I started this blog because I was looking for a way to keep track of places I visited, where I would go again, which place I would avoid and to share my experience. We (me and my family) usually go camping every 3 to 4 weeks, we started when my oldest son was 1 year old. My second son had his first camping trip and road trip from the bay area to Death Valley when he was 6 Month old. If you have questions or comments, feel free to comment, or contact me.
