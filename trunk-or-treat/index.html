Trunk or Treat Carnival Game Inspirations

Creative DIY game ideas and trunk setups for school carnival fun.

✨ Concept ideas illustrated with AI for inspiration

Plinko Trunk Challenge

Drop pucks or balls down the pegged board into score slots. Easy to build with plywood, dowels, and fun carnival banners.

Angry Birds Knockdown Toss

Painted tin cans stacked as green pigs on a shelf. Kids toss plush birds to knock them down for points.

Soccer Goal Challenge

A colorful cutout board with target net cutouts for kicking or tossing mini soccer balls into high-point zones.

Pumpkin Patch Ball Roll

A ramp roll game leading up to a giant jack-o'-lantern target with point holes, framed with hay bales and fall lights.

Circus Elephant Ring Toss

A classic wooden peg ring-toss board featuring an elephant theme and striped carnival prize shelf with rings in a basket.

Pirate Ship Cannonball Bowling

Turn a truck bed into a pirate ship lane! Roll cannonballs down the wooden deck to knock over pirate pins guarding treasure chests.
