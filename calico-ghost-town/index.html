TRIP 02 - MOJAVE DESERT, CA - ~390 MILES - 2-3 DAYS

Calico Ghost Town and Off-Road Trails

An 1881 silver mining ghost town in the Calico Mountains surrounded by some of Southern California's best OHV trails - rock crawls, canyon runs, and desert arches.

DISTANCE

~390 mi

DAYS

2-3 days

VEHICLE NEEDED

Tips & Logistics

Camping Options

Camping right at the ghost town is the move - you're steps from the trails and entry is included.

Calico Ghost Town Campground265 sites below the town — full hookups, showers, fire rings; camping includes ghost-town entry. Reserve via San Bernardino County Parks — holiday/event weekends fill up. BLM Dispersed · Mule / Owl CanyonFree primitive camping on surrounding BLM land — no services, pack everything out. Owl Canyon Campground (~30 min) is the developed budget alternative. Barstow Hotels10 minutes away — chain hotels, gas, groceries. The no-camp fallback.

Activities

Maggie Mine TourDescend into an original 1880s silver mine. Narrow Gauge RailroadRide the Calico & Odessa line around the town. Gold PanningTry your luck on-site. Mystery ShackThe gravity-illusion attraction. Calico–Vegas OHV RunThe annual off-road event, if the timing lines up. Elmer's Bottle Tree RanchQuirky Route 66 folk-art stop en route.

Off-Road Trails & Hikes

Trail ratings and condition updates from Trails Offroad - Calico Region.

Calico Ghost Town Walk0.7 mi · easy · 2–3 hrs — main street, schoolhouse, mine and saloon of the 1881 silver town. Mule Canyon~2 mi RT · easy–moderate · ~1 hr — colorful intro trail; a Kill Bill 2 filming location. Wall Street Canyon~4 mi RT · moderate 4x4 · ~2 hrs — stunning canyon walls with overlook; 4WD and 33" tires. Kramer Arch~3 mi RT · hard 4x4 · ~2 hrs — stone arch just big enough to squeeze a vehicle through; high-clearance 4x4 required. Odessa Canyon~4 mi RT · extreme 4x4 · ~3 hrs — the flagship Calico trail: 7-foot waterfall, rollover spot, tight off-camber canyon. Calico OHV Open RidingMiles of trails · varies · half–full day — ATVs, UTVs, dirt bikes welcome; green/red sticker required.

Tips & Logistics

Entry Fee~$8/adult — free with overnight camping. OHV StickersGreen/red sticker required; ATVs and UTVs allowed. Red-sticker OHVs Oct–Jun only. VehicleHigh-clearance 4x4 needed for Kramer Arch and the canyon trails. Supplies in Barstow3 mi away — gas, groceries and hotel options. Avoid SummerJune–September Mojave heat is brutal — plan Oct–Apr.
