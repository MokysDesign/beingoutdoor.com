Calico Ghost Town and Off-Road Trails

An 1881 silver mining ghost town in the Calico Mountains surrounded by some of Southern California's best OHV trails - rock crawls, canyon runs, and desert arches.

DISTANCE

~390 mi

DAYS

2-3 days

VEHICLE NEEDED

Stock 4x4 (lifted for crawls)

BEST SEASON

Oct - Apr
