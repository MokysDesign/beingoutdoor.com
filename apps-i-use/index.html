Apps I use

These are the phone apps that have actually earned the space on my home screen. I test new ones every trip, and most get deleted. The ones below stayed because they made something easier or safer.

If you have a favorite I should try, drop me a note.

Guru Maps Pro

Offline topo maps. I keep the maps of everywhere I'm going downloaded before I leave cell range. Free version is fine for casual use, Pro is worth it if you do real backcountry or off-road driving.

Packing Pro

Trip packing lists. Yes, it's a packing list app. No, I don't think it's overkill. Per-trip lists, per-person lists, the kind of thing that means I never forget the camp-stove fuel on a 4-day trip.

Paprika

Recipe manager. I run the kitchen planner through here — meal plans, shopping lists, the family recipes. Syncs across phone and laptop. Not specifically a travel app, but on longer trips it's how I keep "what are we eating Thursday" sorted when we're driving.

Why I keep it: [your reason]

Watch Duty

Wildfire alerts. Pulls from official fire sources and pushes notifications when there's a fire within a radius you set. I have it set to 50 miles. Free, no ads, runs on donations.

Why I keep it: [your reason]

Out Groceries

Grocery list and meal planning. Lighter than Paprika for the "we ran out of milk" case. I use Paprika for the trip-level planning and Out Groceries for the running grocery list back home.

Gypsy Guide

Audio tour guides for road trips. Think of it as a personal tour guide in your phone that knows the local history of wherever you're driving. Works offline once downloaded. Covers a lot of US national parks and western road trips.
