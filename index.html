Off-Road Adventures

Trip 01 · Valley of FireNevada · ~520 mi · 3-4 days - blazing red sandstone, petroglyphs, alien desert an hour from Vegas. Any stock SUV or truck · best Oct-Apr. Trip 02 · Calico Ghost TownMojave, CA · ~390 mi · 2-3 days - an 1881 silver ghost town ringed by SoCal's best OHV trails. Stock 4x4; lifted rig only for the crawls · best Oct-Apr. Trip 03 · White Rim RoadCanyonlands, UT · ~850 mi · 4-5 days - the legendary 100-mile mesa loop. HC 4WD + low range required, permit needed, no dogs · best Mar-May & Sep-Nov. Trip 04 · Lost Coast OverlandN. California · ~220 mi · 2-4 days - redwoods, the King Range and beach camps on the remotest coast. High clearance, 4x4 for Usal Rd · late May-Oct.

Camping Set-Ups

Every trip card above says what it really takes - short version: Valley of Fire works in any stock SUV or truck. Calico wants a stock high-clearance 4x4 (a lifted rig with low range only if you're doing the rock crawls). White Rim requires true high-clearance 4WD with low range - park rules, no AWD crossovers. Lost Coast needs high clearance, with 4x4 strongly recommended for Usal Road in the dry season.

TruckPickup with a rooftop tent or bed camper - hauls all the gear and handles every trip on this site. Jeep Rubicon · LiftedLow range, lockers and clearance - the set-up for White Rim and the rock-crawl loops. SUVA stock high-clearance SUV covers Valley of Fire and the main trails - tent camping from the trunk. Camper VanComfort at basecamp - great for the developed campgrounds; leave the rough trails to a 4x4.
