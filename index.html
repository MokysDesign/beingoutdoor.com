Nature

Places I keep returning to — and routes I'm planning. Hubs hold multiple visits; single trips are one trip, done or in the works.

Hub Arches NP Arches NP 2,000+ sandstone arches near Moab — Delicate Arch, Devils Garden, the Windows. Hub PLANNED. Hub Bryce Canyon Bryce Canyon Hoodoos, rim hikes, and the darkest night skies in the National Park system. Hub PLANNED. Planned Calico Ghost Town Mojave, CA · ~390 mi · 2–3 days — an 1881 silver ghost town ringed by SoCal's best OHV trails. Stock 4x4; lifted only for the crawls · best Oct–Apr. Hub Death Valley Death Valley Below sea level, above 11,000 ft — badwater, Titus Canyon, Ubehebe. Hub PLANNED. Hub Escalante · Grand Staircase Escalante Slot canyons, slickrock, and the Hole-in-the-Rock Road. Hub PLANNED. Hub Grand Canyon Grand Canyon South Rim, Bright Angel, the North Rim if you've got the time. Hub PLANNED. Hub Joshua Tree NP Joshua Tree NP Two deserts meet — granite stacks, Joshua-tree forests, and dark-sky camps. Hub PLANNED. Done Lofoten · Norway Lofoten · Norway Sharp mountains falling into the sea, fishing villages with red cabins — one trip, fully done. Planned Lost Coast Overland N. California · ~220 mi · 2–4 days — redwoods, the King Range and beach camps on the remotest coast. HC + 4x4 for Usal Rd · late May–Oct. Planned Mojave Desert Mojave Desert Kelso Dunes, Cinder Cones, the most empty corners of SoCal. Dispersed camping, stock SUV · best Oct–Apr. Planned Valley of Fire Nevada · ~520 mi · 3–4 days — blazing red sandstone, petroglyphs, alien desert an hour from Vegas. Any stock SUV or truck · best Oct–Apr. Planned White Rim Road Canyonlands, UT · ~850 mi · 4–5 days — the legendary 100-mile mesa loop. HC 4WD + low range required, permit needed · best Mar–May & Sep–Nov. Hub Yellowstone & Grand Teton Yellowstone & Grand Teton Returning place — geysers, wildlife, and the Tetons a short drive south. Hub PLANNED. Hub Yosemite A place I keep coming back to — hikes, sections, and seasons, added one visit at a time. Planned Zion Zion The chain-aided spine hike (Angels Landing, permit required), the Narrows wading hike, and Canyon Overlook. Park shuttle gets you to every trailhead.

City

Cities are hubs by default — returning places, even after one visit. Done cities are visited; planned cities are on the board.

Hub Berlin & Beyond A place I keep returning to — history, street-level Berlin, and the day trips that make each stay different. Hub Boston Boston Walkable history — the Freedom Trail, harbor islands, and the start of a New England road trip north. Hub Dublin Dublin Done — pubs, the coast, and a great launchpad into Ireland's west. A city I'll come back to. Hub Las Vegas Las Vegas Basecamp city — drive in, sleep there, drive out to Valley of Fire, Death Valley, Grand Canyon, Zion. The Southwest launchpad. Hub London & Beyond A place I keep returning to — landmarks, markets, museums and day trips across London and beyond. Hub Los Angeles Los Angeles Neighborhoods, beaches, and the drives between them — LA rewards a car and a few extra days. Hub Munich Munich Bavaria's capital — beer halls, the Englischer Garten, and day trips into the Alps. Hub Oslo Oslo A city that opens straight into fjord and forest — the launchpad for a Norway trip. Hub Paris Paris Done — neighborhoods over landmarks, day trips to Versailles and Giverny. A city I'll come back to. Hub Salzburg Salzburg Done — old town, the river, and the surrounding lakes and mountains. A city I'll come back to. Hub San Diego San Diego Best weather in the country — Balboa Park, the coast, and easy access to the desert in under two hours. Hub San Francisco San Francisco Done — neighborhoods, day trips down the coast, and the drives that make it worth more than a weekend. Hub Seattle Seattle Pike Place, ferries, and the Olympics on the doorstep — a basecamp for rainforest and mountain day trips. Hub Tokyo Tokyo Neighborhoods over landmarks — Shibuya, Shimokitazawa, day trips to Kamakura and Hakone. Hub Vienna Vienna Done — cafés, music halls, and the Wienerwald on the edge of town. A city I'll come back to. Hub Zurich Zurich Done — old town, lake, and the Alps an hour away. A city I'll come back to.
